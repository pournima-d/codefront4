# BATON — Features Guide & Tutorial

## Key Features
1. **Clean Empty-Start Workspace**: Starts with zero pre-loaded demo research data. Only login accounts are available for quick role testing, alongside real self-service account registration (`Create Account`).
2. **Private vs. Public File Vault (`Files & Vault`)**:
   - Choose **Private (Only Me)** or **Public (Workspace Shared)** *before* uploading PDF, Markdown, TXT, CSV, or JSON files.
   - Private files require your authenticated session and are completely hidden from every other team member.
   - Toggle visibility anytime with **Make Public** / **Make Private**, download files, or delete files you own.
3. **Word-Style Rich Text Notes Studio (`Notes Studio`)**:
   - Full formatting ribbon: Font Family, Font Size, Headings (`H1–H3`), Quote & Code blocks, Bold/Italic/Underline/Strikethrough/Superscript/Subscript, Text & Highlight colors, Alignment, Bullet & Numbered Lists, Indentation, Checklists, 3x3 Tables, Hyperlinks, Undo/Redo, and Clear Formatting.
   - Live Word & Character counts, Private vs. Public access toggle, and one-click `.doc` export.
4. **Live Team Status, Roles & Work Tracker (`Team & Work`)**:
   - Live `ONLINE` / `AWAY` / `OFFLINE` status updated via 15s heartbeat and SSE.
   - Displays each team member's Role and exact work counts (`Files`, `Notes`, `Claims`, `Reviews`, `Handovers`, `Total work`).
   - Live **Who Has Done What** feed showing recent contributions across the team.
5. **Claims, Review Queue, Redactions, Debt Radar & Handover**:
   - Reconstruct typed claims from Public files linked verbatim to exact sections.
   - Reviewer approval queue, automatic sensitive-data masking, Debt Radar scoring, custom Handover builder, and dynamic Cold-Start quiz.

## 5-Step Quick-Start Tutorial
1. **Sign In or Create Account**: Register a new account with your chosen role on the Home page, or use a Demo Login button to explore roles immediately.
2. **Upload Files**: Go to **Files & Vault**, select **Private (Only Me)** or **Public (Workspace Shared)**, and drop or paste your files.
3. **Write Notes**: Open **Notes Studio**, format your document using the Word-style toolbar, pick Private or Public, and click **Save note** (or export `.doc`).
4. **Track Team Work**: Open **Team & Work** to check who is online/offline, each member's role, work volume, and the chronological contribution feed.
5. **Review & Hand Over**: Extract claims from Public files, approve records in **Review Queue**, confirm redactions, and publish a verified brief in **Handover**.
